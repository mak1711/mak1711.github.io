Hello, I'm Mohamad

I'm a PhD researcher at the American University of Beirut. I combine robot learning with physics-based models so legged robots can sense contact, stay balanced and move over rough ground, first in simulation and then on real hardware.

I train learning-based estimators and controllers in PyTorch, including graph networks, Transformers and reinforcement-learning residuals, and I work across MuJoCo, Isaac Sim and Gazebo. I build the optimisation and MPC that sit around them, and I take the results from Gazebo onto Unitree Go1 and Go2 robots with motion capture and force sensing to check them. My first-author journal paper was co-written with five colleagues, and most of my lab projects are team efforts.

Before my PhD, I spent a year as a research assistant in AUB's robotics lab. Before that, I did a master's in robotics and intelligent systems with UTC Compiègne and the Lebanese University.

Away from the robots, you'll find me on a mountain trail, behind a camera, or on the farm with my goats, sheep and chickens. I like things that walk, and I like to understand how they do it.

Research

My published work, and what I'm working on now.

Published · 2026

Efficient and Robust Standing Postures of Quadruped Robots

Advanced Robotics Research · e202500178 · first author · with H. Jomaa, J. Challita, K. Zaidan, R. Saab and H. Hussein

A robot standing still under a load still burns energy in every motor. This work models the robot's kinematics and static equilibrium, calibrates its mass and sensors, and estimates the external load. It then spreads the torque across the four legs within friction and contact limits and adjusts the body posture to match. A worst-case search checks every direction the load could come from.

Tested in Gazebo and on a physical Unitree Go1.

Sim to hardware

How a project moves from an idea to a robot on the ground. Every step here comes from my own projects.

  1. 01

    Model

    Kinematics, static equilibrium and rigid-body dynamics, with contact and friction constraints written in from the start.

    Posture paper · MPC
  2. 02

    Simulate & learn

    Generate training data in simulation (Gazebo, MuJoCo, Isaac Sim) on flat ground and slopes. Train graph networks, Transformers and RL residual policies in PyTorch.

    Estimation · jump-down
  3. 03

    Debug the physics

    Tune contact and integration settings, find solver infeasibility, and learn where a controller starts to slip before trusting any number.

    Trajectory opt. · MPC
  4. 04

    Deploy

    Run it on Unitree Go1 and Go2 over ROS / ROS 2, with ONNX inference on CPU and optimisers re-solving live at about 10 Hz.

    Go1 · Go2 hardware
  5. 05

    Measure

    Check against motion capture, force and pressure sensing, and calibrated payload tests, then report what ran in simulation and what ran on hardware.

    OptiTrack · 3 kg payload
